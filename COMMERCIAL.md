# Commercial use of EditMax

EditMax is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). This page explains in plain
words what that means. If anything here differs from the license, the license wins.

## Free, no key needed

- **Personal use:** editing your own videos for study, hobby projects, family, private entertainment or amateur
  pursuits, with no commercial use in mind.
- **Noncommercial organizations:** charities, schools and universities, public research, public safety and health,
  environmental groups and government bodies, whatever their funding.
- **Noncommercial open source:** reading, changing and sharing the code, and building noncommercial projects on it, as
  long as you pass on the license and its `Required Notice:` line.

## Needs a commercial license

- Using EditMax at or for a company, including freelancers and agencies doing client work.
- Making videos you are paid for or that earn money: client projects, ads, sponsored or monetized content.
- Selling EditMax, bundling it in a paid product, or offering it as a hosted service.
- Building a commercial product on its code, including a fork.

## Getting a license

A commercial license comes with a license key that proves the purchase; terms and prices are on request. To get one, open an issue
titled **Commercial license** at <https://github.com/SkidGod4444/editmax/issues>, or contact the maintainer through
<https://github.com/SkidGod4444>. Say who will use it, how many people, and what for.

## Parts with their own licenses

EditMax bundles components that stay under their own terms, and nothing above changes those terms. They are listed in
[THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
