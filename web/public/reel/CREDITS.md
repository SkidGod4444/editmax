# Demo footage

Stock clips from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, attribution not required; credited here anyway).
Every file on the site was cut, graded and exported by EditMax from the 3840x2160 originals, then encoded for the web.

- `tiles/`: 4 s loops, 640 wide, for the curved wall. Teal & Orange on the bike clip; contrast and vibrance on the rest.
- `reel/parkour.mp4`: the same parkour cut at 1280 wide, for the captions card.
- `hero/`: one 6 s surf ride exported three times by EditMax, as `teal` (Teal & Orange), `original` and `bw` (Black & White), 1440 wide.
  The page's look switcher crossfades between them.
- `stills/`: single frames from those same EditMax exports.
- Posters and stills are WebP.
